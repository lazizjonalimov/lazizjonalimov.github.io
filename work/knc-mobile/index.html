The challenge

Visitors explore 16 trails on their own. They needed a guide in their pocket: where they are, what is around them and what is happening at the center that day.

The approach

We built the app in Flutter and Dart, so one codebase ships to both iOS and Android. Interactive trail maps use the Google Maps API, QR codes at historic sites open the story of each spot, and real-time event updates keep visitors in the loop.

Key deliverables

  • Cross-platform Flutter app
  • Interactive trail maps for 16 trails
  • QR scanning for historic sites
  • Real-time event updates

The outcome

The app shipped and serves the center’s visitors, increasing engagement with self-guided visits.
