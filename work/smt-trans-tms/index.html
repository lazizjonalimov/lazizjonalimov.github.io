Transportation Management System

Aug 2025 – Feb 2026

Project details:

A custom TMS that brought SMT Trans’s fleet, driver and compliance data into one place — and put compliance deadlines on autopilot.

Client:
SMT Trans · Philadelphia, PA
Industry:
Transportation & logistics
Role:
IT Systems & Data Specialist
Stack:
Python · Flask · PostgreSQL · REST APIs · Excel

On this page

The challenge The approach Key deliverables The outcome

The challenge

Running a trucking company means keeping track of trucks, drivers and a steady stream of compliance documents that each expire on their own schedule. Keeping that information current by hand took a large share of the team’s administrative time, records drifted out of step between systems, and a missed renewal is a real regulatory risk.

The approach

I built a Python application on Flask and PostgreSQL, with REST APIs, so fleet, driver and compliance records live in one central system. Around it, scheduled Python scripts and Excel logic — formulas, validation and triggers — follow each document through its lifecycle and send reminders before anything is due. Data synchronization and validation pipelines keep the internal systems agreeing with each other.

Key deliverables

  • Central fleet, driver and compliance database
  • Flask application with REST APIs
  • Automated compliance reminders
  • Document lifecycle tracking
  • Data sync and validation pipelines

The outcome

The team spends far less time on paperwork, the data is more trustworthy, and regulatory deadlines no longer slip through the cracks.
