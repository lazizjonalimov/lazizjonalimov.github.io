The public website for a nationwide transportation company, and a full-stack Flask app that takes quote requests and new clients online.

The challenge

Customers needed a clear picture of what SMT Trans offers — its services, fleet and how to work with the company — and a simple way to ask for a quote. Driver recruiting needed a front door too.

The approach

The website covers services, the fleet, driver applications and contact options for customers across the U.S. For quotes, I designed and developed a full-stack Flask web application — Jinja2 templates, JavaScript, RESTful APIs and SQLAlchemy data models — that handles online quote requests and client intake.

Key deliverables

  • Company website: services, fleet, contact
  • Driver application flow
  • Online quote request form
  • Client intake with SQLAlchemy data models
  • RESTful API layer

The outcome

Quote requests now arrive online, ready to act on, and inbound leads went up noticeably.
