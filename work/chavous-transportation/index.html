The challenge

A carrier that hauls across the whole continental U.S. needs to look as dependable online as it is on the road — and shippers, drivers and partners each need a quick way to reach the right place.

The approach

I built the site with Next.js and React and deployed it on Vercel. Pages are server-rendered, so they load quickly and are easy for search engines to read. The structure routes each visitor to what they came for: freight quotes, driver applications or dispatch contact.

Key deliverables

  • Freight quote requests
  • Driver application path
  • Dispatch contact
  • Server-rendered pages for speed and SEO
  • Hosting and deployment on Vercel

The outcome

Chavous has a modern, quick-loading site that is built to be found in search and sends every visitor to the right next step.
